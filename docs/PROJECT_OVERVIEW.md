# Project Overview: Home Visit Buddy System

## Core Mission
The **Home Visit Buddy System** is a dedicated safety platform designed to protect Occupational Therapists (OTs) during community visits. It digitizes the "Buddy System" protocol, ensuring that every therapist leaving the department for a home visit has a designated buddy and a clear safety trail monitored by clerical staff and colleagues.

## Problem Solved
Manual safety logs are prone to errors and delays. This app solves the "Loss of Contact" risk by:
- Enforcing mandatory check-ins at specific intervals.
- Providing real-time status updates (e.g., "Arrived," "Finished").
- Alerting buddies and clerks if a therapist fails to call back within the 15-minute safety window.
- Standardizing risk assessments before any visit begins.

## Target Audience
- **Case Therapists**: Users who perform home visits and record their progress.
- **Clerks**: Admin staff responsible for monitoring therapist departures and initial safety check-ins.
- **Buddy Therapists**: Colleagues assigned to monitor a therapist's safety, especially during out-of-office hours.

## Current Status
The project is a functional MVP with core workflows for **COT (Community Occupational Therapy)** and **CGAT (Community Geriatric Assessment Team)** visits. It includes a robust authentication layer, profile approval system, and a detailed case management dashboard.
